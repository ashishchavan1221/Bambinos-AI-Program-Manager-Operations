# Operations Dashboard

## Dashboard Metrics

1. Total paid invoices
2. Pending faculty forms
3. Unapproved renewal requests
4. Unscheduled bookings
5. Invoices pending for more than 12 hours
6. Batch capacity status
7. Total exceptions
8. Automation hours saved

## Dashboard Users

- Head of Operations
- Operations Team
- Faculty Team

## Objective

Provide real-time visibility into student renewals
and identify delays in the scheduling process.
